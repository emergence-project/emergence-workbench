\section{Coloring with five colors}\label{sec:five-color}
If $G-v$ is colored with five colors, the neighbours of $v$ use all five, and $v_3 \notin \kc{1}{3}{v_1}$, then
after swapping the two colors on $\kc{1}{3}{v_1}$ the vertex $v$ can take the color $c(v_1)$.
(For the structure theorem see \cite{doe2004structure}; for Kempe swaps see \cite{lorem1988sufficiency}.)

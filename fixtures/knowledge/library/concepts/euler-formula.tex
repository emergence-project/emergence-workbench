\section{Euler formula}

\subsection*{정의}
$V - E + F = 2$ for every connected plane graph.

\subsection*{핵심 성질·정리}
Edge bound: $E \le 3V - 6$ when $V \ge 3$, with equality iff $G$ is a triangulation.

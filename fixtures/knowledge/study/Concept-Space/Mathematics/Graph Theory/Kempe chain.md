A Kempe chain $K_{ij}(v)$ collects the vertices reachable from $v$ along vertices colored $i$ and $j$. It rests on [[Euler formula]] and [[Graph coloring]], and swapping its two colors keeps a coloring proper.

> [!theorem] Swap lemma
> If $w \notin K_{ij}(v)$, then swapping $i$ and $j$ on $K_{ij}(v)$ leaves $c(w)$ unchanged.

Used in [[Planar graph coloring]] and [[Unavoidable set]].

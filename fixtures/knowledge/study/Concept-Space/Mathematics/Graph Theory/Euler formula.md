Euler's formula relates the vertices, edges and faces of a connected plane graph. It is the counting step behind [[Graph coloring]] bounds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V - E + F &= 2 \\
E &\le 3V - 6 \quad \text{(simple graph, } V \ge 3)
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!note] Low degree
> Hence every [[Planar graph]] has a vertex of degree at most five, and a [[Kempe recoloring]] extends a coloring of $G - v$ to $G$.
